# Section 16 — User Flows

## Flow A · Federation chair reviews the federation health

1. Lands on `overview`. Sees 4 associations, 942 total members, CHF 218,400 circulated.
2. Notes Lausanne Cultural Circle is "Late on dues 2 months".
3. Taps it → routes to that association's hub for follow-up.

## Flow B · Onboarding a new association

1. From `associations`, taps **+ Link association**.
2. Enters the target association ID and reads the cascading policy summary.
3. Sends invite to the target's president.

## Flow C · Reviewing consolidated finance

1. Opens `consolidated`. Tab: Dues.
2. Reviews dues compliance grid. Two associations late.
3. Taps **Send reminder** → opens Section 6 audience preset for late payers.

## Edge states

- **Association leaving the federation**: `associations` shows a banner with the planned exit date and outstanding obligations.
- **Federation in setup phase**: overview shows a checklist (bylaws ratified, at least 3 associations linked).
