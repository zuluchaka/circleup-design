# Community & Social Specification

## Overview
Community engagement and social features that strengthen trust, encourage savings behavior, and drive organic platform growth. Members earn recognition through achievements, participate in savings challenges, refer friends for rewards, and celebrate milestones together—fostering the social bonds that make ROSCAs successful.

## User Flows

### Referral Program
- Generate unique referral links for sharing via SMS, WhatsApp, email, or social media
- Track referral status (invited, joined, active) on referral dashboard
- Receive rewards when referrals join circles and make first contribution
- View multi-level referral earnings and network growth

### Achievements & Recognition
- Earn badges for savings milestones (first contribution, 100% on-time, circle completion)
- View progress toward next badge with clear requirements
- Display earned badges on member profile
- Share achievements to social media with celebratory animations
- Get featured in member spotlight for outstanding participation

### Savings Leaderboards
- View leaderboards filtered by circle, association, or platform-wide
- Opt-in/opt-out of public visibility
- See category rankings (total saved, streak length, on-time percentage)
- Celebrate top savers with recognition notifications

### Savings Challenges
- Browse and enroll in savings challenges (30-day streak, double contribution, etc.)
- Track progress against challenge goals with visual indicators
- Compete on challenge-specific leaderboards
- Earn special badges and rewards for challenge completion

### Milestone Celebrations
- Receive personalized celebration screens for savings milestones
- Share milestones with network (first payout, 1-year anniversary, savings goal reached)
- Privacy controls for what's shared publicly
- View milestone history and achievements timeline

### Community Events
- Organizers create in-person or virtual events (meetups, celebrations, workshops)
- Members RSVP and receive event reminders
- Share photos and memories post-event
- Event calendar integration

### Peer Mentorship
- Experienced savers opt-in as mentors
- New members matched with mentors based on savings goals and language
- In-app messaging for mentorship communication
- Mentor recognition badges for helping others succeed

### Social Accountability
- Publicly commit to savings goals within circle or network
- Accountability partner pairing for mutual support
- Progress visibility to accountability partners
- Encouragement notifications when partners contribute

### Community Impact
- View collective platform statistics (total saved, members helped, circles completed)
- Association-level impact dashboards
- "Together we've saved X" celebratory milestones
- Social proof notifications ("15 members contributed this hour")

### Success Stories
- Submit personal savings success stories
- Featured stories on discovery pages (with consent)
- Inspire others with real member testimonials
- Story categories (first home, education, emergency fund)

## UI Requirements
- Referral dashboard with shareable link, tracking table, and earnings summary
- Achievement badge gallery with progress indicators and unlock animations
- Leaderboard views with rank cards, opt-in toggle, and category filters
- Challenge cards with enrollment button, progress bar, and countdown timer
- Milestone celebration modal with confetti animation and share buttons
- Event cards with RSVP status, calendar export, and attendee count
- Mentor matching interface with profile cards and request workflow
- Community impact banner with animated counters and milestone celebrations
- Success story carousel with member photos and quotes
- Social proof toast notifications for real-time community activity
